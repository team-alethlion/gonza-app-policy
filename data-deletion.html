Account & Data Deletion Instructions

Information on how to request the deletion of your account and personal data across Gonza Systems services.

1. Gonza Printer Mobile App (On-Device Storage)

The Gonza Printer mobile app operates completely locally on your device and does not store account records on external cloud servers.

To delete all printer pairings, local settings, and cached preferences stored by the Gonza Printer app:

  1. Open your device's Settings → Apps → Gonza Printer.
  2. Select Storage → tap Clear Data and Clear Cache.
  3. Alternatively, uninstalling the app permanently erases all locally saved preferences.

2. Gonza Systems Web Platform & Cloud Accounts

If you have registered an account on the Gonza Systems business cloud platform, you have the full right to request permanent deletion of your profile, business records, and transaction logs.

How to Request Cloud Account Deletion:

  • Send an email to info@gonzasystems.com or gonzasystems@gmail.com.
  • Use the subject line: Account Data Deletion Request - [Your Business/Account Email].
  • Our support team will verify your account ownership and permanently erase all associated user records, credentials, and data within 30 days.

3. Retention Requirements

Some financial records (such as tax invoices and payment processing logs) may be retained for statutory audit periods as mandated by applicable Ugandan tax and commercial accounting regulations before complete permanent purge.
