Data & Account Deletion Instructions

Provider: Gonza Systems  |  Policy: User Data Rights & Deletion  |  Effective Date: August 29, 2026  |  Location: Kampala, Uganda

1. Overview and User Rights

Gonza Systems values your privacy and provides full transparency regarding user data rights. In compliance with Google Play Store developer guidelines, GDPR, and international data protection laws, this page outlines the procedures for clearing local device data and requesting permanent deletion of user accounts.

2. Gonza Printer Mobile Application (On-Device Storage)

The Gonza Printer mobile application functions entirely as a local hardware bridge on your device and does not maintain cloud database user accounts for printing.

To permanently erase all locally stored printer preferences, paired Bluetooth devices, and cached settings:

  1. Open your device's Settings > Apps (or App Management) > select Gonza Printer.
  2. Select Storage & Cache.
  3. Tap Clear Data (or Clear Storage) to instantly erase all local application state.
  4. Alternatively, uninstalling the application from your device permanently removes all associated private storage.

3. Gonza Systems Cloud Platform (Cloud Account Erasure)

If you have an active registered business account on the Gonza Systems web platform, you have the right to request a complete and permanent erasure of your account credentials, business profiles, and associated data.

Step-by-Step Procedure to Request Account & Data Deletion:
  1. Send an email from your registered account email to info@gonzasystems.com or gonzasystems@gmail.com.
  2. Specify the subject line: Account Data Deletion Request - [Your Registered Email].
  3. Include your business name and confirmation that you wish to permanently purge all data associated with your tenant.

4. Processing Timeline & Data Retention

Verification & Execution: Upon receiving your written request, our security and compliance team will verify your account ownership and complete the permanent deletion of your account records across all active databases within thirty (30) calendar days.

Statutory Retention: Where required by applicable Ugandan financial accounting and tax regulations, certain transactional records (such as tax invoices or statutory payment confirmations) may be archived for legally required audit periods before final destruction.
