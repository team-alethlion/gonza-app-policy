Privacy Policy

Application: Gonza Printer  |  Developer: Gonza Systems  |  Effective Date: August 29, 2026  |  Location: Kampala, Uganda

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Gonza Systems ("we", "us", or "our") handles information and permissions in connection with the Gonza Printer mobile companion application (the "Application").

Gonza Printer is a hardware companion service engineered to facilitate local communication and formatting of receipt data between point-of-sale systems and ESC/POS-compatible thermal receipt printers over Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and local network connections.

2. Information Collection and Privacy Standards

Gonza Printer does not collect, record, transmit, harvest, or sell any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or customer records.

The Application functions strictly as an offline-first hardware driver bridge. We do not maintain user accounts on external cloud servers specifically for this printer driver application, nor do we intercept, inspect, or log receipt printing content for commercial purposes.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

To perform thermal receipt printing and establish hardware connections, the Application requires access to specific device features under standard Android operating system permissions:

  • Bluetooth Permissions (BLUETOOTH, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN): Required exclusively to scan for, pair with, and transmit raw ESC/POS binary print commands to nearby thermal receipt printers. Location-related Bluetooth flags are configured strictly for hardware discovery (never for user tracking).
  • Local Network & Wi-Fi Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE): Required exclusively to transmit print commands to network-connected thermal receipt printers (LAN / Wi-Fi IP printing) on the local area network.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE): Required to maintain an active, reliable connection with the thermal printer during active business hours and prevent Android from terminating print jobs mid-transaction.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to display ongoing printer connectivity status and alerts regarding print job completion or transmission errors.

4. Data Processing & Local Storage

Volatile In-Memory Processing: All receipt text, item lines, amounts, and customer receipt layouts received for printing are processed in temporary volatile memory and routed directly to the connected printer. No print payload is stored persistently or transmitted to external parties.

Local Preferences: The Application stores non-personal hardware preferences (such as the MAC address of your paired printer, paper width formatting of 58mm or 80mm, and character encoding settings) locally on your device using Android's private SharedPreferences storage.

5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The Application does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking cookies, or commercial analytics trackers. No data generated during printing operations is shared with or accessible to third-party vendors.

6. Children's Privacy

The Application is a commercial point-of-sale hardware utility designed for business use and is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Security Practices

We prioritize the security and integrity of your printing transactions. All data transfers between your mobile device and the thermal printer occur across direct, local communication channels (Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi subnet) within your immediate physical or network perimeter.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect updates in operating system requirements or hardware standards. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated "Effective Date" at the top of the document.
