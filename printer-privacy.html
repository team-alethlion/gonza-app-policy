Privacy Policy for Gonza Printer

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Gonza Systems ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how the Gonza Printer mobile application (the "Application") handles information when you use our companion thermal receipt printing service.

2. No Collection of Personal Information

Gonza Printer does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or customer data to external servers or third parties.

The Application functions as a local, on-device utility bridge between your business point-of-sale platform and ESC/POS thermal receipt printers.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

To connect with thermal printer hardware and format receipts, the Application requests the following standard Android permissions:

  • Bluetooth Permissions (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN): Used solely to discover nearby Bluetooth POS printers and send receipt print commands.
  • Local Network & Wi-Fi (INTERNET, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE): Used solely to transmit print data to network-connected (LAN/Wi-Fi) thermal printers on your local network.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE): Used to maintain an active print queue in the background without dropping communication during active business hours.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to notify users of printer connectivity state and print job status.

4. Data Processing & Local Storage

All receipt and invoice data (e.g., item descriptions, prices, business totals) is processed in volatile device memory and directly transmitted to your selected printer. No print payload is analyzed, harvested, or sent to external servers.

Your printer configuration (such as selected printer MAC address, paper width of 58mm/80mm, and character encoding) is stored strictly locally on your device via Android private storage.

5. Third-Party Services & Tracking

  • No Ad Networks: The Application contains no third-party advertisements or ad-tracking SDKs.
  • No Behavioral Tracking: We do not monitor, profile, or track your activity across other applications or websites.

6. Children's Privacy

The Application is intended as a commercial business tool and does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
